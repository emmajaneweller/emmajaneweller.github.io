I use cosmological simulations to study the coevolution of massive black holes and their host galaxies. My papers have investigated wandering massive black holes, AGN feedback, and the $M_{BH} - M_\star$ relation. You can view them on my [ADS](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/public-libraries/2XODRSogRt69IPwZkt9RqQ)/[SciX](https://scixplorer.org/public-libraries/2XODRSogRt69IPwZkt9RqQ) library.
